# User Flows

## 1. New User

```text
Landing
↓
Register
↓
Optional Profile Setup
↓
Discover Anime
↓
Open Anime
↓
Add to Planning / Watching
↓
Anime appears in list
```

Tidak ada onboarding panjang yang wajib.

## 2. Watching Progress

```text
Homepage
↓
Continue Watching
↓
Frieren 14 / 28
↓
+1
↓
15 / 28
```

User tidak perlu membuka anime detail hanya untuk memperbarui episode.

## 3. Anime Completion

```text
Progress reaches final episode
↓
Confirm completion
↓
status = COMPLETED
↓
completion date recorded
↓
optional score
↓
statistics update
↓
achievement evaluator
↓
public activity if enabled
```

## 4. Discovery

```text
Discover
↓
Filter
↓
Anime Results
↓
Anime Detail
↓
Planning
```

Discovery juga dapat berasal dari Studio, Staff, Character, Season, dan Recommendation.

## 5. Seasonal

```text
Season
↓
Current Season
↓
Anime
↓
Add to Planning
↓
Anime starts airing
↓
Watching
↓
Schedule tracks next episode
```

## 6. Rewatch

```text
Completed Anime
↓
Start Rewatch
↓
REWATCHING
↓
progress resets
↓
finish
↓
repeatCount + 1
↓
COMPLETED
```

## 7. Achievement

```text
User Action
↓
Tracking Update
↓
History Event
↓
Achievement Evaluator
↓
Progress Update
↓
Condition Met
↓
Unlock
↓
Notification
↓
Optional Showcase
```

## 8. Social

```text
Follow User
↓
Public Activity Appears
↓
Like / Reply
↓
Notification
↓
Conversation
```

Social tetap secondary terhadap anime tracking.
